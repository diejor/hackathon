# SafeSense: 2-minute demo script

**Target length:** 2:00. The narration is about 290 words; read it slowly and calmly.

**Before recording:**
- Gemini billing is on.
- Use a fresh browser profile on the live site (https://diejor.github.io/hackathon/), at phone size or on a real phone.
- Krishna's iPhone is linked (LINK code) and screen recording.
- AI budget for the whole recording: about 10 chat questions, including retakes.

**⚠️ Practice texts allow 1 a day.** Krishna's PRACTICE test *is* the footage, so screen-record it on the iPhone the first time.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Title card, then the Home dashboard | "Last year Americans lost $470 million to scams that started with a text. Warnings make people anxious. They don't make people better at spotting scams. SafeSense does: pause, check, then act." |
| 0:12–0:22 | Home: type a name in "What should we call you?", then Hello {name}, the mascot and points, the three games, The Phone Call locked until 2 points | "No sign-up, no timers, no pressure. Just tell it your name. Every game you finish earns a point, and your helper grows with you." |
| 0:22–0:40 | Scam or Not?: one scam (the lookalike web address) and one genuine text, each with its explanation | "In Scam or Not, you decide, then see the signs. Read a web address from the right: this one isn't your carrier. Some messages are genuine. Being careful is never a mistake." |
| 0:40–1:00 | Role Reversal: choose a deadline and a $2.99 fee; Priya checks her order history and forwards to 7726; the debrief | "In Role Reversal, you play the scammer. Pick your tactics and watch a careful person take them apart. Every trick becomes a defense in the debrief." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Chat with us: add a screenshot of a toll scam; the verdict, signs and next step appear | "Something worrying on your phone? Chat with SafeSense: type it, say it, or add a screenshot. Gemini reads it and gives a verdict, the signs and what to do, and it remembers what you've missed in the games." |
| 1:20–1:45 | iPhone: forward a scam text and the reply arrives; then PRACTICE, the labeled practice text, REPORT, and the debrief | "Or just text SafeSense over iMessage, through Photon. The same calm answer comes back in seconds. Reply PRACTICE and we send a clearly labeled fake scam to practice on, opt-in only, with STOP always honored." |
| 1:45–2:00 | The architecture slide or README diagram, then the Home page | "It's all one SpacetimeDB module: private tables, signed Photon webhooks and Gemini calls inside the database. SafeSense is a companion to Scam Shield, for the messages that still get through." |

**Backup plan:**
- If Gemini is slow, cut the 1:00 segment to a pre-recorded take.
- If Photon fails, use Krishna's earlier screen recording, or show the web "Text SafeSense" card and say the line is limited to one registered phone.

**Recording tips:**
- Phone at 100% zoom, in light mode.
- Hide notifications (Do Not Disturb).
- Record each segment separately and cut them together. Keep the raw takes.
