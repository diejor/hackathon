# SafeSense: 3-minute video script

**Order:** Krishna, then Diego, then Emily. Everything is spoken over the live demo, about 420 words.

**Recording:**
- Record the screen first, following the [cues], then record the voices over it.
- Each person can record separately and redo lines as often as needed.
- Speed up slow screen moments in the edit (the phone ringing, Gemini "thinking").

**Before recording:**
- Use a fresh browser (incognito) on https://diejor.github.io/hackathon/.
- Have the toll-scam screenshot saved on the phone.
- Make sure Krishna's iPhone is linked and ready for the call.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb.

---

## Krishna: the problem and the games (0:00–1:15)

[Home dashboard. Type a name in "What should we call you?"]
"Last year, Americans lost 470 million dollars to scams that started with a text message. Most advice is a list of warnings, and warnings make people anxious. They don't make people better at spotting scams. SafeSense is a calm place to practice."

[Scam or Not?: answer one, show the signs]
"In Scam or Not, you read a real-looking text and decide. Then you see the signs that gave it away."

[Role Reversal: pick a trick, then the debrief]
"In Role Reversal, you play the scammer. You pick the tricks, and you watch a careful person take them apart. Every trick becomes a lesson."

[The Phone Call unlocks, press Call me, the phone rings]
"Every game earns a point. At two points, The Phone Call unlocks. SafeSense calls your phone with a practice scam call. Spot it and you earn a point. Get fooled and you lose one."

## Diego: Chat with us and texting (1:15–2:00)

[Chat with us: add the toll screenshot]
"Some messages still get through.
So you can ask SafeSense.
Type it, say it, or add a screenshot."

[The answer appears]
"Gemini reads it.
It tells you if it looks like a scam,
and what to do next."

[iPhone: a forwarded text and the reply]
"You can also text SafeSense from your phone,
and get the same answer back."

(Let the reply stay on screen for a second.)

## Emily: design and close (2:00–2:50)

[Aa text size, the dashboard, the mascot]
"We designed SafeSense for people who feel unsure about technology, often older adults. There are no timers and no pressure. The text is large, and you can make it larger. Your progress grows with a little helper as you practice."

[Home]
"SafeSense works alongside tools like Scam Shield. Those block scams at the network. SafeSense helps with the ones that still get through. Pause. Check. Then act. Thank you."
